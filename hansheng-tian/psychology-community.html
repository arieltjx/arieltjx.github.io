03 / UNDERSTANDING PEOPLE

Learning
to listen.

My interest in psychology connects to a simple question: how can we understand one another better?

CLASS PSYCHOLOGY REPRESENTATIVE

Psychology in
our classroom.

As my class’s psychology representative, I led a mini-lesson about listening and communication.

We used a message-passing activity: one student reads a sentence, then whispers it to the next person. It gave us a concrete way to explore how information can change as it travels.

I want to keep learning about psychology and use that knowledge to contribute to my school community.

PUBLIC SPEAKING / 2025

Speaking up for the environment.

I gave a talk at the final evaluation of Beijing's 2025 environmental education activities for primary and secondary school students.

It was a chance to share my interest in environmental science with others.
