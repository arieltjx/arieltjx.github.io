I’m Hansheng Tian, a student at Beijing Sanfan Middle School. Robotics has been part of my life for years, but there is much more I want to explore.

I’m curious about how things work and how people think. I like turning questions into experiments, staying active through sports, and sharing what I learn with other people.

VEX has given me something to keep working at over many seasons. Science projects let me try out ideas that might help people around me. Sport, magic, and psychology give me other ways to learn and connect.

This is a collection of the things I’m working on, learning from, and growing into.
