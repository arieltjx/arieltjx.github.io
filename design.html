01.   SOFT 

- Maya
- Cinema 4D
- Redshift
- Adobe Suite
"SOFT" encapsulates a project that transcends traditional visual representation to evoke tactile sensations through digital media. With each render, I aim to infuse a sense of tangibility into the visual experience—inviting the audience to not only see but feel the essence of softness and fluidity. Different textures, such as balloon-like simulations, glass, fluid, and plastic are meticulously animated with squashes, stretches, falls, and inflations to mimic the dynamism of real-life interactions. Light and environment play a crucial role, in enhancing the realism of materials and the emotions they are designed to elicit. This project is a symphony of sensory cues, carefully composed to create an intimate connection with the viewer, evoking a palpable response to the softness portrayed within a captivating digital environment.

02.    Cyberpunk Room

- Maya
- Cinema 4D
- Redshift
- Adobe Suite
For "Cyberpunk Room," I aim to meticulously craft a 3D model that transcends mere aesthetics to become a vibrant tableau of cyberpunk life. This endeavor focuses on weaving together detailed textures, evocative atmospheric lighting, and fluid animations to transform a digital space into an immersive spectacle. My goal is to not just create a room, but to foster an environment that mesmerizes and engages, offering a glimpse into a neon-drenched, futuristic world. Through this project, I aspire to elevate digital design, making the cyberpunk room not just a visual treat but an ornate, living experience that captivates the senses and imagination.

03.    <So Far>

- Final Cut Pro
- Adobe Premiere
"So Far" combines collage and narrative techniques to portray the array of natural and man-made disasters humanity faces—wars, floods, earthquakes, tsunamis, diseases, and more. Through real-life testimonials, the film reconstructs the profound impact and enlightening lessons these calamities impose on people. It emphasizes the theme of cherishing everything we currently have, urging viewers to appreciate the present amidst the chaos and unpredictability of life. This project is a visual and emotional journey that invites reflection on resilience, gratitude, and the human spirit's capacity to endure and learn.

04.    <Solitude>

- Final Cut Pro
- Adobe Premiere
"Solitude" is a deeply personal exploration shot entirely in New York City, capturing a period of introspection during a challenging phase of my life. This solo journey delves into the complex emotions surrounding solitude, presenting it not as a state of loneliness but as an essential phase for self-discovery and personal growth. Through this film, I navigate the tumultuous journey from noise to silence, listening to the profound voices within. It underscores the necessity of becoming at peace with oneself as a precursor to fostering healthy relationships with others, embodying the concept of self-consistency and the transformative power of embracing solitude.

05.    ElderNet

- Figma
- Sketch
ElderNet helps elderly Chinese immigrants overcome cultural adjustment, language barriers, and social isolation. The program enhances quality of life through cultural integration, community building, and healthcare access. Services include language classes, cultural events, system navigation workshops, and wellness activities. Chinese-language mental health support includes counseling and meditation. The platform builds connections through intergenerational activities and digital training, empowering immigrants to thrive in their new home while maintaining cultural traditions.

06.    FavorPath

- Figma
- Sketch
 The project uses olfactory memory and electronic scent technology to enhance the dining experience for children with autism spectrum disorders who struggle with eating disorders. Our goal is to help children with ASD overcome dietary challenges related to sensory sensitivities and make mealtimes more comfortable. By releasing familiar scents, we aim to reduce food-related anxiety, encourage diverse food choices, and improve the overall well-being of children with ASD.
